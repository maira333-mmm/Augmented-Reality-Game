# 📖 About

The **Augmented Reality Food Adventure Game** is an innovative software development project that creates an immersive AR experience combining the virtual and physical worlds. This project aims to provide users with a realistic experience of traveling around the world, experiencing different cultures, and enjoying a wide range of cuisines—all through augmented reality.

## 🎯 Objective

Our goal is to offer users an engaging, educational, and enjoyable experience by replicating the sensation of consuming food from different parts of the world, making it an immersive culinary journey.

---

# ✨ Key Features

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| 🌍 **Realistic Virtual World** | Visually attractive virtual environment replicating real places and cultures |
| 🍽️ **Food Interaction** | Engage with and consume food products in virtual environment |
| 🌮 **Multi-Cuisine Options** | Variety of cuisines and dishes from around the world with cultural background |
| 👃 **Sensory Experience** | Replicate flavor, texture, and aroma of food |
| 👥 **Multiplayer & Social** | Virtual dining journeys with friends and sharing experiences |
| 🛒 **Virtual Commerce** | Buy and sell virtual products through user-friendly marketplace |
| 🔒 **Privacy & Security** | Secure online transactions and data protection |
| 📱 **Cross-Platform** | Works on smartphones, tablets, and AR glasses |
